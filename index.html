Create a self-lock trustless · self-service

Lock your own tokens for your own wallet, directly — no owner approval, no owner transaction, no owner involvement of any kind. Because you are both the depositor and the beneficiary, this is trustless by construction: the moment your transaction confirms, the schedule is permanent. It can never be cancelled or edited by the contract owner, any future owner, or anyone else — the only way these funds ever move is your own claim() calls, on the exact schedule you set here.

Create self-lock

Pulls totalAmount of the chosen token from your connected wallet and locks it for your connected wallet to claim later. A flat 1% fee is deducted immediately and sent to the fee address — only the remaining 99% is credited to your lock.

🔒 This cannot be undone. There is no cancel button for a self-lock — not for you, not for the contract owner, not ever. Double-check the token, total amount, per-claim amount, and frequency before confirming.

Top up my self-lock

Adds funds to your own existing lock's remaining balance — whether it was created here or by the owner on your behalf — without changing its schedule. No owner involvement needed: you approve and top up directly. A flat 1% fee is deducted immediately and sent to the fee address.

Owner-only actions. Every call here reverts on-chain if the connected wallet isn't the owner — buttons are disabled locally as a convenience, so no wasted gas. If you're a customer setting up your own lock, use the "Create a self-lock" section under the Claim tab instead — it needs no owner involvement.

Connect the owner wallet to enable these actions.

Create lock

Each beneficiary can hold one active lock per token — the same address can have an independent USDT lock and a WMATIC lock at once, but not two locks in the same token. Pulls totalAmount of the chosen token from your wallet on confirm. A flat 1% fee is deducted immediately from totalAmount and sent to the fee address — only the remaining 99% is credited to the beneficiary's lock.

Self-lock rule (v9, trustless): if the beneficiary address below is the contract owner's own address, this lock becomes a permanent, trustless self-lock the moment it's created — neither cancelLock nor updateLockParams will ever work on it again, for this owner or any future owner. If a beneficiary wants to lock their own tokens for themselves with no owner involvement at all, point them to the Create a self-lock section under the Claim tab instead — that path (createSelfLock) always produces the same kind of permanent, uncancellable lock, for anyone, without you ever touching their funds. Locks you create here for someone else are unaffected and stay fully cancellable/editable as usual.

Top up

Adds funds to an existing lock's remaining balance without changing its schedule. A flat 1% fee is deducted immediately from this amount and sent to the fee address — only the remaining 99% is credited to the beneficiary's lock. Must match the token the lock was created with.

Update claim amount / frequency

Changes the schedule going forward. Does not affect what's already been claimed. Reverts on a self-lock (beneficiary's own address at creation, or created via self-service createSelfLock) — those schedules are fixed permanently and trustlessly, for the owner and everyone else, forever.

Cancel lock

Immediately deactivates the lock and sends the entire remaining balance to the refund address. Cannot be undone. Reverts on a self-lock (beneficiary's own address at creation, or created via self-service createSelfLock) — those can never be cancelled by the owner, by design. That's what makes a self-lock trustless: it's not "the current owner promises not to," it's the contract itself refusing the call, permanently, no matter who holds ownership.

Rescue surplus

Recovers token balance that isn't owed to any beneficiary — either surplus above what's locked for a token already in use, or the entire balance of a token that's never been used in a lock at all (e.g. sent here by mistake). Works for any ERC-20 address; cannot touch funds owed to beneficiaries. v7 merges the old separate "rescue other token" action into this one — the contract computes the exact sweepable amount for you, so there's no amount field to get wrong.

Transfer ownership

Hands admin control of the entire contract — every lock — to a new address. Cannot be undone from here.
